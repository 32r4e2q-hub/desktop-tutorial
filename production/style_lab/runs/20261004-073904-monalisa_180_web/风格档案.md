## 实际读取范围

- 抽帧 **48** 张，均匀覆盖整段（避开首尾各 0.5 %）；不是逐帧审查。
- 硬切粗检测：阈值 0.24，解码宽度 320px，估算 **33** 次显著画面切换，中位间隔 5.77 秒。淡入淡出与机内运动可能漏检或误检，此值仅供节奏参考。
- 运动幅度：10 组相隔 0.4 秒的帧对，算灰度平均绝对差。
- 音轨只探测是否存在，**不克隆、不迁移**；参考片的画面一帧都不会进新片。
- 仅从成片无法确认原作者用了哪款生成或剪辑软件，不把推测写成结论。

## 迁移到新片（只迁风格，不迁素材）

- 上面每一条判定都已经在 `style-candidates.json` 里翻译成英文提示词片段，三份候选共用同一套调色/光线/颗粒词，差别只在画面定位与人物政策；
- 中文姓名、日期、字幕一律后期添加，不交给视频模型拼写（手册第 1 步）；
- 真实人物只以背影、剪影、手出现，受害者不出人像，不展示遗体与侵害过程；
- 全片常驻「AI动画情景重现 · 非新闻影像」角标，法庭/取证/搜查镜头换成更具体的标签；
- 参考片的**剪辑节奏只作参考**：新片仍按手册 45 镜 × 4 秒网格与 `clause_times.py --check-cuts` 走。

## 三组 STYLE_PREFIX 候选

### 1. 超写实 3D· 人物只给背影/剪影/手部（`photoreal_silhouette`）

画面按参考片的调子做成实拍级 3D CGI：材质、光线、镜头运动都往实拍靠，但沿用手册护栏——真实人物不出清晰正脸，全片常驻「AI动画情景重现 · 非新闻影像」角标。推荐度最高：既吃满参考片的质感，又不碰内容红线。

风险：无新增风险，与 production/gilgo 的护栏一致。

```
Photorealistic 3D CGI cinematic recreation that reads like live-action photography, with physically plausible materials, global illumination and true-to-life scale, [本片场景：地点 + 年代 + 关键道具，开工时替换]: near-black, sepia brown, olive, dim, moody exposure, warm amber-tungsten colour temperature, punchy high-contrast grade, clean crisp optics, visible fine 35mm film grain, flat even focus across the frame, handheld, energetic camera movement; horizontal 16:9 cinematic composition, one single continuous smooth slow camera move per shot exactly as directed; every character is shown only from behind, in silhouette, or as hands and props - never a clear frontal face; no likeness of any real person;  absolutely no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, license plates or brand marks anywhere inside the frame; no gore, no blood, no corpse, no violence, no nudity. 
```

### 2. 超写实 3D· 允许清晰人脸（`photoreal_faces`）

同样按参考片做实拍级 3D，但允许画面出现清晰的普通人脸（仍然不是任何真实人物的肖像）。人物表情、眼神能大幅提升叙事密度，代价是畸变与「像真人新闻影像」的观感风险一起上升。

风险：**违反手册护栏**（PRINCIPLES：真实人物只以背影、剪影、手出现）。若采用，必须改写 build_story.py 的 PRINCIPLES，并把「AI动画情景重现 · 非新闻影像」角标加粗常驻；涉受害者的镜头仍不出人像。

```
Photorealistic 3D CGI cinematic recreation that reads like live-action photography, with physically plausible skin, materials, global illumination and true-to-life scale, [本片场景：地点 + 年代 + 关键道具，开工时替换]: near-black, sepia brown, olive, dim, moody exposure, warm amber-tungsten colour temperature, punchy high-contrast grade, clean crisp optics, visible fine 35mm film grain, flat even focus across the frame, handheld, energetic camera movement; horizontal 16:9 cinematic composition, one single continuous smooth slow camera move per shot exactly as directed; ordinary non-celebrity human faces are allowed when the story needs a face, but never a likeness of any real public figure or victim;  absolutely no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, license plates or brand marks anywhere inside the frame; no gore, no blood, no corpse, no violence, no nudity. 
```

### 3. 写实 3D 动画电影质感（`cinematic_3d`）

偏 3D 动画长片：体积光、干净的材质、轻微风格化。比纯写实更好控——Agnes 生成人脸和手时畸变概率明显更低，复审重做的镜头数通常最少，代价是离参考片的「实拍感」差一档。

风险：无新增风险；与参考片的写实度有落差，需要你确认接受。

```
Stylized-realistic 3D animated feature look with soft volumetric light, clean physically based materials and gentle stylization, rendered like a high-end 3D animated film, [本片场景：地点 + 年代 + 关键道具，开工时替换]: near-black, sepia brown, olive, dim, moody exposure, warm amber-tungsten colour temperature, punchy high-contrast grade, clean crisp optics, visible fine 35mm film grain, flat even focus across the frame, handheld, energetic camera movement; horizontal 16:9 cinematic composition, one single continuous smooth slow camera move per shot exactly as directed; every character is shown only from behind, in silhouette, or as hands and props - never a clear frontal face; no likeness of any real person;  absolutely no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, license plates or brand marks anywhere inside the frame; no gore, no blood, no corpse, no violence, no nudity. 
```
